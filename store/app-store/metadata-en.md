### Description (English)

```
Mekteb brings mosque education to your phone — for teachers, parents,
students and mosque administrators.

For teachers
- Manage groups and record attendance easily
- Assign homework to a whole group or individual students
- Document weekly notes and progress
- Plan, run and grade exams
- Message parents and the mosque

For parents
- Follow your children's attendance, homework and progress
- View the lesson plan and calendar
- Receive messages and announcements from the mosque

For students
- Your own groups, homework and attendance
- Lesson materials and notes
- Quran reader with recitation, saved verses and hifz progress

For the mosque administration
- Manage students, teachers and parents
- Attendance, exams and annual report
- Announcements, messages and notifications
- Enrollment and account creation (no public registration)

Privacy first: all data belongs to the respective mosque, is never sold to
third parties and is protected by access controls. Suitable for children's
education — no ads, no third-party tracking.

Note: an account assigned by the mosque is required to use the app.
```
